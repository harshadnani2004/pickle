The 4-Step Sun-Curing Ritual

Unlike factory pickles that rely on synthetic acetic acid and fast pasteurization, we let time, whole spices, and raw sunlight perform their natural culinary alchemy.

01

Hand-Inspected Green Mangoes

Procured straight from farmers in Andhra and Tamil Nadu. Only stone-hard raw mangoes with pristine sour pulp make the cut.

02

Stone-Ground Guntur Chillies & Methi

Spices are hand-roasted on slow flame and coarsely ground in traditional stone mills so the essential aromatic oils are preserved.

03

Wood-Pressed Sesame (Gingelly) Oil

Golden cold-pressed oil extracted without heat chemicals covers the pickles, acting as nature’s purest antioxidant and seal.

04

21-Day Porcelain Jar Maturation

Jars are tied with bleached cotton cloth and sunned for three weeks. Every day, the batch is gently stirred by hand with a neem ladle.

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions regarding shelf-life, shipping, and ingredients? We’ve got answers.

Our pickles have a natural shelf life of 12 months! Because we follow the ancient method of preserving with natural sun-dried rock salt and keeping the pickle submerged under a protective layer of pure cold-pressed sesame/mustard oil, bad bacteria cannot survive. Always remember to use a dry clean spoon!

We strictly use 100% wood-churned (wood-pressed / Kachi Ghani) sesame (gingelly) oil for South Indian style pickles (Avakaya, Gongura, Tomato) and pure cold-pressed mustard oil for Northern varieties. We NEVER use cheap refined palm oil, cotton-seed oil, or synthetic preservatives.
