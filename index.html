Ammamma's 4 Sacred Kitchen Rituals

Sun-Cured In Porcelain Martabans (Pickles)

Stone Mortar & Pestle Pounding (Podis & Karams)

Dry-roasted country curry leaves, roasted dals, flaxseeds, and whole garlic cloves stone-ground to preserve vital nutrients and explosive natural aroma.

COMMON QUERIES

Everything You Need to Know

Have questions regarding shelf-life, ingredients, and packaging? We’ve got answers.

Every category is prepared naturally without chemical preservatives:
• Veg & Non-Veg Pickles: 6 to 12 Months (sun-cured with natural sea salt and cold-pressed oil; keep clean dry spoon).
• Pindi Vantalu & Sweets: 25 to 45 Days (Dry Fruits Putharekulu: 30 days in airtight box; Bobbatlu: 7-10 days).
• Hot Snacks: 45 Days in airtight containers.
• Podis & Karams: 4 to 6 Months (store in cool dry glass or steel jars).

We use 100% Pure Desi Cow Ghee for Pindi Vantalu (Ariselu, Sunnundalu, Putharekulu, Bobbatlu, Kajjikayalu) and wood-pressed (Kachi Ghani) sesame and groundnut oils for our pickles and hot snacks. We NEVER use cheap palm oil, dalda, vanaspati, or artificial food chemicals.

Pickles are packed in leak-proof jars with induction seals; Pindi Vantalu and Hot Snacks are heat-sealed in food-grade moisture-barrier pouches and rigid outer boxes; Podis are sealed in aroma-lock packs. Everything is cushioned with biodegradable transit packaging.
